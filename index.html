Introducing Zerodash

Most software projects stall. Agencies hand back a deck instead of a product. Off-the-shelf tools almost fit, then quietly cost you a headcount in workarounds. And the AI pilots never leave the sandbox.

Zerodash builds what your business actually runs on. We design, ship, and operate custom solutions and AI agents that plug into your real systems: your CRM, your ledger, your inbox, and everything in between.

Approach

Start small. Ship fast.
Keep going.

We do not spend months writing a specification. We find the highest-value problem, get a useful version into your hands quickly, and improve it with your team from there.

  1. Find the problems that create value

    A short discovery with the people who know the work. We look for the places where a change would make a real difference, and start there.

  2. Build a working version

    A real product or agent connected to your systems, not a deck. Usually in your hands within days.

  3. Test it with your team

    Your people use it on real work. We watch what breaks, what gets skipped, and what they ask for next.

  4. Iterate and operate

    We keep shipping after launch, run what we build in your cloud, and hand over clean code and docs at every step.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Everything teams usually ask before their first product or agent build. Anything else, book a call.

Can AI solutions integrate with our existing systems?

Yes. We can integrate AI solutions with your existing enterprise applications, APIs, databases, knowledge systems, IoT platforms, and other data sources.

Is our data secure?

Security and data privacy are built into the solution architecture. Access, permissions, data handling, and deployment can be designed around your organization's security and compliance requirements.

Will it work reliably at scale?

Yes. Solutions are designed with scalability in mind, from an initial proof of concept to production deployments across users, products, business units, and regions.

How do you ensure AI outputs are accurate and reliable?

We combine trusted enterprise data, grounding, validation, guardrails, and human oversight where required to make AI outputs more reliable and relevant to your business.

Can AI work with our specific products and processes?

Yes. AI solutions can be designed around your products, workflows, terminology, business rules, and domain-specific requirements rather than relying solely on generic AI capabilities.

How quickly can you build and launch?

We focus on getting a useful version into your hands quickly, not spending months writing a specification. We start with the highest-value problem, build it, test it with your team, and iterate.

What if we don't have a technical team?

That's fine. You don't need to know how to build the solution. We handle the technical work and work directly with the people who understand your business.
