What do you build?

Custom software and AI agents that improve processes, improve workflows, and increase revenue or reduce costs. Products, platforms, automation and the agents inside them.

How does an engagement start?

Tell us your problems. We run a short discovery with your team, identify the gaps, and build only what closes them.

What is a Forward Deployed Engineer?

An engineer who sits with your team end to end: identifies the problem, tests solutions, builds, and deploys in your stack.

Do we have to replace the tools we already use?

No. The ZeroDash Platform connects the tools you already pay for. When a tool doesn’t exist, we build it on the same platform.

Which industries do you work in?

Logistics, manufacturing, real estate, e-commerce, retail and customer support. If the problem is operational, it likely fits.

How fast will we see something working?

Days for a first working version, not months. We test it with your team and keep shipping after launch.

Is our data secure?

Everything runs in your own cloud accounts with scoped access. You own the code and the data.
